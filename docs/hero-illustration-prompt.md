Create an ultra-premium, highly detailed 3D glassmorphism illustration specifically designed to be integrated into the **RIGHT SIDE of Shifana's personal portfolio website Hero Section**.

A stylish, intelligent female software developer wearing:
- Elegant black hijab with realistic soft fabric details.
- Premium white futuristic tech jacket.
- Modern round transparent glasses.
- Soft lavender glowing headphones.
- A confident, thoughtful and friendly expression.

She is seated at a sophisticated minimalist workstation, working on a sleek silver laptop. Her posture should look natural, professional and confident.

### Surrounding 3D Technology Elements

Surround the character with beautifully designed floating 3D technology objects:

1. Translucent floating glass coding windows containing subtle code snippets.

3. A glowing lavender and icy-blue AI neural network brain visualization.
4. Floating terminal and developer code editor interfaces.
5. Premium miniature analytics dashboard with graphs and charts.
6. A cute, sophisticated white AI robot assistant floating near the laptop.
7. Small floating 3D planets, digital orbs and sparkling particles.
8. A neat stack of miniature developer books labelled React, JavaScript, Python and System Design.
9. Subtle futuristic digital globe with interconnected network points.
10. Delicate holographic interface elements and glass floating panels.

### Visual Style

Premium 3D CGI, luxury technology brand aesthetic, sophisticated glassmorphism, realistic materials, smooth rounded edges, translucent glass surfaces, soft reflections, delicate shadows, beautiful ambient lighting and highly detailed 3D objects.

The illustration must feel polished, elegant, creative and professionally art-directed rather than cartoonish or childish.

- Pure white
- Soft lavender
- Pastel purple
- Icy blue
- Light mint
- Subtle violet neon

Use bright, airy lighting. Avoid black or dark backgrounds, harsh neon effects and oversaturated colours.

- Wide horizontal 16:9 composition.
- Position the female developer slightly towards the centre-right.
- Keep the laptop and main character clearly visible.
- Distribute floating technology objects naturally around her.
- Maintain a balanced composition without overcrowding.
- Ensure the character, laptop and major elements are completely within the frame.
- Leave sufficient breathing space around the composition.
- Design the illustration to sit inside an existing rounded glassmorphism Hero Stage panel on a dark midnight portfolio website.
- The illustration itself must remain bright, soft and luminous, creating beautiful contrast against the website's dark background.
- Ensure the edges blend naturally with the surrounding website design.
- The illustration must work responsively on desktop and tablet layouts.

### Image Quality & Output

- Transparent PNG background with genuine alpha transparency.
- No rectangular background.
- No separate website mockup or complete webpage.
- No borders or frames around the image.
- No watermark or brand logo.
- No unnecessary text or random unreadable typography.
- Crisp edges and ultra-detailed rendering.
- High-resolution professional 3D CGI output.
- Preserve the visual clarity of the character even when displayed at a smaller hero-section size.

### Overall Creative Direction

Imagine a premium futuristic technology brand campaign featuring an intelligent female full-stack developer. Combine elegant character design, futuristic AI elements, sophisticated glass interfaces and beautiful soft 3D lighting.

**The final result should feel like a custom-designed hero illustration for a premium female software engineer's portfolio — futuristic, intelligent, elegant, innovative and visually unforgettable.**
